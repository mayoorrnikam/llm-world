LLM World

The evidence-backed history of large language models.

Releases, families, labs, capabilities, context windows and pricing — every figure traced to the lab's own announcement, paper, model card or documentation. Where a lab publishes nothing, the record says so instead of guessing.

/

Why trust this record

  • Primary sources

    Official announcements, papers, model cards and documentation. Reporting alone is never enough to mark a date verified.

    The evidence rules

  • Nothing is guessed

    A figure no lab published stays empty. Undisclosed figures and unresearched ones are recorded as different things, because they are.

    What is missing

  • Claim-level evidence

    Dates, context windows and parameter counts are traced to the specific source that states them, not to a source list.

    Open any record

  • Citations that cannot rot

    Sources are cited by dated snapshot. A live page proves what it says today, not what it said when it was read.

    How sources are cited

  • Corrections in public

    When the evidence changes, the record changes and the change is published — including the figures this project got wrong.
